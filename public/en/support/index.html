Help and contact

Something not working, or have an idea? Write to soporte@vocify.es. Telling us your device and what you were doing helps us fix it sooner. (The app is in Spanish; we answer in Spanish or English.)

Frequently asked questions

Which files can I import?

Scores in MusicXML (.musicxml, .xml, .mxl) and MIDI (.mid), and audio recordings (WAV, MP3, M4A, AAC, OGG, OPUS, FLAC, AIFF…). From MuseScore: File → Export → MusicXML. On iPhone you can also open a score or audio from WhatsApp, Mail or Files with “Share → Vocify”.

How do I turn a recording into voices?

Tap Import and pick the audio. Say how many voices can be heard (or let Vocify guess) and wait a moment: the analysis runs on your device. It works best with voices alone (a cappella), no instruments and little reverb. The result is a starting point: fix any wrong notes in the editor.

If each singer records their part separately (all starting at the same point), import the first one and then the others choosing Add to “…” as a new voice: they end up together in one song, and come out much better than the whole group on a single track.

What works best is Blend recording and score (in the menu, or by picking both files at once): the notes come from the score, which are right, and the timing, key and octave come from the recording. It can also add a voice with what is actually sung, and keep the recording playing alongside the voices.

How do I rehearse just my voice?

In the voices panel, tap S (solo) on your voice or turn the others down. The rehearse button (⇄) loops a few bars, with metronome, count-in and slow speed.

How do I share songs with my group?

Create an account, go to Menu → Group and account, create the group and send the invite code. Then, with the song open, tap Share “…” with the group in that same panel. Changes sync automatically.

Do I need an Internet connection?

Not to view, play, edit or hum: all of that works offline. Audio conversion always works offline in the app; in the browser it needs a connection the first time, to download the model. Sharing with the group and the AI assistant need a connection.

No sound on iPhone

Turn the volume up and check no voice is muted in the voices panel. The iPhone app plays even with the silent switch on; in the iPhone browser, turn silent mode off.

Is there an AI limit?

Yes, a number of requests per person per day, so the service can stay free. The assistant features that don’t use AI have no limit.

How do I download my data or delete my account?

In Menu → Group and account: Download my data or Delete my account. More details on deleting your account.
