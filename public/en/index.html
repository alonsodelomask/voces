Your group’s voices, as lines of light.

Upload a score or a recording and Vocify draws every voice as a neon line that rises and falls with the melody. Hear your part, mute the others and rehearse until it clicks.

Open Vocify in your browserHow it works

Free. Works without an account. iPhone app coming soon. The app is in Spanish.

🎼 Import the score

MusicXML (MuseScore, Finale, Sibelius, Flat…) or MIDI, with repeats, ties and chords.

🎙️ Or a recording

WAV, MP3, M4A, OGG, FLAC… Vocify listens to the audio on your device and turns it into voices you can then fix.

🌈 See every voice

Each voice is a coloured line with its name. See at a glance who goes up, who goes down and where you cross.

🎧 Rehearse yours

Solo or mute per voice, slow speed, metronome, count-in and loops for the tricky bars.

✏️ Edit and compose

Add, move and stretch notes with your finger; change chords, transpose and undo. Or hum a tune and it becomes notes.

👥 Share with your group

Create the group, invite with a code and songs sync for everyone.

🤖 Arranging assistant

Checks crossings and leaps, suggests chords and writes new voices. With AI (Claude) for richer ideas.

🎸 Chords for instruments

Optionally shows how to play each chord on guitar, ukulele or piano.

📱 Video for social media

Record a vertical video with the lines and the sound, ready to share.

Your songs are yours

No ads and no tracking. Without an account everything stays on your device; microphone audio and recordings are analysed right there and never uploaded. Privacy policy.
