Delete your Vocify account

From the app (fastest)

  1. Open Vocify (on iPhone or in the browser) and sign in.
  2. Go to Menu → Group and account (“Grupo y cuenta”).
  3. Tap Delete my account (“Eliminar mi cuenta”) and confirm.

If you want to keep a copy, tap Download my data (“Descargar mis datos”) first.

If you can’t sign in

Email soporte@vocify.es from your account’s address with the subject “Delete my account”. We will delete it within one month and confirm it to you.

What is deleted

  • Your email, password and display name.
  • Your membership of all groups.
  • The AI usage log.
  • Sign in with Apple access, which is revoked with Apple.

What remains

  • Songs stored on your device (they stay there; delete them in My songs or by uninstalling the app).
  • Songs you shared with a group, which remain available to its members without your name. If you don’t want them to stay, remove them from the group first (My songs, ⊘ button). If you were the only member, the group and its songs are deleted.
