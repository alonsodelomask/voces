Privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This is a translation; if there is any discrepancy, the Spanish version prevails.

In short: you can use Vocify without an account, and then everything stays on your device. The microphone and the recordings you import are analysed on your device and never uploaded. If you create an account, we keep only what the group features and the AI need. No ads, no tracking, and we don’t sell data. You can download your data and delete your account from the app.

1. Controller

The controller is pending: owner name, tax ID pending: tax ID, address pending: address (“Vocify”, “we”). For any privacy matter, write to soporte@vocify.es.

2. What data we process

Using Vocify without an account

Your songs and settings are stored only on your device (browser or app storage). We receive nothing.

Microphone (“Hum”)

With your permission, the app listens to the microphone to detect the pitch of the notes you sing. The audio is analysed in real time on your device, never saved to a file and never sent to any server. Only the resulting notes are stored in your song.

Recordings you import

When you turn an audio file (WAV, MP3…) into voices, the analysis runs on your device with a model shipped with the app. The audio file is not uploaded anywhere.

If you create an account (optional)

  • Email address and, if you use one, your password (stored hashed by our authentication provider; we can’t see it).
  • Display name, visible to the members of your groups.
  • If you use Sign in with Apple: the identifier Apple gives us, the email Apple shares (it may be a private Apple relay address) and your name the first time. We also keep, encrypted, an Apple code used only to revoke that access when you delete your account.
  • The groups you belong to, your role (creator or member) and the invite code.
  • Songs you share with a group (title, notes, chords, voice names and colours) and who last changed them.
  • AI usage: the date and time of each request, only to enforce the daily limit.

AI assistant

When you ask the AI something, we send your request and a text description of the open song (notes, chords, voice names and tempo) to Anthropic, which provides the Claude model, to generate the answer. We don’t send your email, your name or any audio. The assistant’s “Review”, “Chords” and “Create voices” features run on your device and send nothing.

Technical data

Like any Internet service, our hosting providers automatically log technical data about each connection (IP address, date, page requested, browser type) to keep the service secure and fix errors. We don’t use it to profile you.

5. Transfers outside the EU

When you use the AI assistant, your request is processed in the United States. In its data processing agreement Anthropic commits to the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission (Decision 2021/914). Other US-based providers likewise apply GDPR safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses or other valid mechanisms) for any access from outside the EU.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account, name and groups: while you have the account. They are deleted with it.
  • AI usage log: 30 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Apple code: until you delete your account and the access is revoked.
  • AI requests at Anthropic: deleted by Anthropic within 30 days, unless flagged for usage-policy violations (up to 2 years) or required by law. They are not used to train its models.
  • Shared songs: they belong to the group. If you delete your account or leave the group, the songs you shared remain available to the other members, without your name. You can remove them from the group beforehand in My songs (⊘ button). A group and its songs are deleted when its last member leaves.
  • Technical connection data: for the limited time our providers keep it for security.

7. Download your data and delete your account

In the app: Menu → Group and account.

  • Download my data gives you a file with your account, your groups and the shared songs.
  • Delete my account deletes your email, name, group memberships and AI usage log, and revokes Sign in with Apple if you used it. Songs stored only on your device are not touched.

If you can’t get into the app, follow the steps to delete your account.

8. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability by writing to soporte@vocify.es from your account’s email address. We will reply within one month. If you think we haven’t handled your request properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or your local authority.

9. Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). The database enforces row-level access rules: each person can only read their own groups and those groups’ songs. Server keys never leave the server, and the Apple code is stored encrypted.

10. Ads and tracking

Vocify shows no ads, uses no third-party analytics, doesn’t track you across apps or websites and uses no tracking cookies. It only uses the device’s local storage for your songs, settings and session.

11. Children

You must be at least 14 (or have your parents’ or guardians’ consent) to create an account. Without an account the app collects no personal data.

12. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, let you know in the app.
